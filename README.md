# Open Text Collections #8
## Even texts

This collection features 26 texts from two different dialects of the Even language, spoken in Siberia.

The texts were narrated by Ija Krivoshapkina, Ekaterina Krivoshapkina, Marta Krivoshapkina, Raisa Alekseeva, Zoja Stepanova, Evdokija Adukanova, Pelageja Banakanova, Gennadij Solodikov, Valentina Axmetova, Evdokija Adukanova, Raisa Solodikova, Rimma Egorova, Natalija Tylkanova, and Artёm Solodikov,  and compiled by Brigitte Pakendorf.

The files in this repository contain the texts in the CLDF format. A human readable version of
the book is available under the name "I always migrated by reindeer: Lamunkhin and Bystraja Even narratives about their traditional way of life" at https://zenodo.org/records/18682200
